The pymupdf parser uses PyMuPDF4LLM, which is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0).

When you need a commercial license

  • You distribute docpipe or a derivative as part of a proprietary SaaS without source disclosure.
  • You cannot comply with AGPL network-copyleft obligations.
  • You need indemnification or enterprise support from the copyright holder.

Recommended defaults

docpipe keeps pymupdf opt-in: install docpipe-sdk[pymupdf] explicitly, or block it with DOCPIPE_DISABLED_PLUGINS=pymupdf. Fast-tier parsing defaults to markitdown instead.
